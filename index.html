Our Mission

Mobilizing students to
serve Charlotte

At the One Love Initiative, we believe students have the power to create real, lasting change. Our mission is to mobilize young people across Charlotte to organize donation drives, assemble O.L.I Kits, and deliver essential supplies to individuals and families facing hardship.

We don't operate independently — we partner with established organizations who share our commitment to community, ensuring that every kit we assemble reaches the people who need it most, efficiently and with care.

"We began as students who asked a simple question: what can we actually do? The answer is the One Love Initiative."

01

Donation Drives

We organize student-led drives at schools, places of worship, and neighborhoods across Charlotte. Community members come together to contribute essential items that form the foundation of every O.L.I Kit.

Student volunteers gather to sort and assemble donated items into O.L.I Kits — carefully packed bundles of essential supplies ready for distribution to families in need.

03

Community Partnerships

By partnering with established nonprofits like Crisis Assistance Ministry, we ensure every O.L.I Kit reaches the families who need it most — efficiently, respectfully, and with care.

Our Story

The One Love Initiative began with a simple question: what can students actually do to help? Founded by Prithivi Vijayakumar, O.L.I was built on the belief that young people don't need to wait to make a difference.

Starting with a single hygiene kit drive in Charlotte, NC, we set out to prove that organized, student-led action — backed by strong community partnerships — can create real, measurable impact.

Our goal is 20,000 O.L.I Kits distributed by the end of summer 2025. We're just getting started.

Our Mission

What We Do

From collection
to community

Every O.L.I Kit starts with a drive and ends with a family in need. Here's how we make it happen.

1

Identify Needs

We work with Crisis Assistance Ministry to identify the specific items families in Charlotte need most, ensuring every drive is targeted and impactful.

2

Organize Drives

Students coordinate and lead donation collection efforts at schools, places of worship, and neighborhoods across Charlotte.

3

Assemble Kits

Volunteers come together to sort and assemble donations into O.L.I Kits — ready-to-distribute bundles of essential supplies.

4

Deliver to Partners

Completed O.L.I Kits are handed off to Crisis Assistance Ministry for direct distribution to families experiencing hardship.

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about the One Love Initiative, our drives, and how you can get involved.

What is the One Love Initiative?
+
The One Love Initiative is a student-led nonprofit based in Charlotte, NC, dedicated to assembling and distributing O.L.I Kits — bundles of essential supplies — for individuals and families experiencing hardship across our community.
What is an O.L.I Kit?
+
An O.L.I Kit is a carefully assembled bundle of essential items donated and packaged by our student volunteers. Our current kit is the Children's Hygiene Kit, which includes a kids toothbrush, toothpaste, kid-friendly soap or body wash, and kid-friendly lotion.
Who receives the kits?
+
All O.L.I Kits are distributed through our partner organization, Crisis Assistance Ministry, which serves individuals and families facing financial hardship in Mecklenburg County, NC.
How can I donate items?
+
We are currently collecting kids toothbrushes, toothpaste, kid-friendly soap, and lotion for our Children's Hygiene Kit Drive. Reach out to us at oneloveinitiative.official@gmail.com to find out where to drop off donations.
Can I donate money instead of items?
+
Yes! Financial donations go directly toward purchasing bulk supplies for O.L.I Kit assembly. You can donate securely through our Stripe donation page — click the Donate button in the navigation bar.
How do I volunteer?
+
We'd love to have you! Volunteers help organize drives, assemble kits, and spread the word. Email us at oneloveinitiative.official@gmail.com or fill out the contact form on our Contact page to get started.
Can I start a drive at my school or organization?
+
Absolutely. We welcome students, school clubs, religious organizations, and community groups to host their own drives. We provide full guidance on how to organize and contribute. Reach out to us to get started.
How are donations used?
+
100% of donated funds go toward purchasing supplies for O.L.I Kit assembly and covering operational costs for our drives. We maintain full transparency in how funds are used.
Where is the One Love Initiative based?
+
We are based in Charlotte, North Carolina, and currently serve families across the greater Charlotte and Mecklenburg County area.
How do I partner with the One Love Initiative?
+
If your organization would like to partner with us — whether as a distribution partner, sponsor, or community collaborator — please reach out at oneloveinitiative.official@gmail.com. We'd love to connect.

Still Have Questions?

We'd love to hear from you. Reach out and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

Our Legal Status

The One Love Initiative is a student-led community initiative — not yet a standalone 501(c)(3). Direct donations to OLI through Stripe are not tax-deductible. However, through our fiscal sponsorship arrangement with Crisis Assistance Ministry — a registered 501(c)(3) — donors can receive a full tax deduction.

✓ Tax Deductible

Donate through
Crisis Assistance Ministry

CAM is our fiscal sponsor and a fully registered 501(c)(3). Donations made through them, designated for the One Love Initiative, are fully tax-deductible and go directly toward our kits.

Step-by-step

1
Go to crisisassistance.org/donate
2
Fill in your donation amount and details
3
In the "Designation" or "Notes" field, write: One Love Initiative
4
Complete your donation — CAM will earmark funds for OLI and issue a tax receipt

Transparency

How your money
is used

🧴

Supplies

The majority of funds go directly toward purchasing hygiene items — toothbrushes, toothpaste, soap, and lotion — in bulk for kit assembly.

📦

Assembly

A small portion covers packaging and materials needed to put kits together efficiently and neatly for distribution.

🚗

Logistics

Minimal funds cover transportation and coordination costs for delivering assembled kits to Crisis Assistance Ministry.

📊

Operations

We keep overhead at an absolute minimum. No salaries, no office. Just students, supplies, and community.

Questions about how funds are used? Email oneloveinitiative.official@gmail.com. We maintain full records of every expense.
