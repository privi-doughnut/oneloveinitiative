Our Mission

Mobilizing students to
serve Charlotte

At the One Love Initiative, we believe students have the power to create real, lasting change. Our mission is to mobilize young people across Charlotte to raise funds, assemble O.L.I Kits, and get essential supplies to individuals and families facing hardship.

We don't operate independently — we partner with established organizations who share our commitment to community, ensuring that every kit we assemble reaches the people who need it most, efficiently and with care.

"We began as students who asked a simple question: what can we actually do? The answer is the One Love Initiative."

Our Story

The One Love Initiative began with a simple question: what can students actually do to help? Founded by Prithivi Vijayakumar, O.L.I was built on the belief that young people don't need to wait to make a difference.

Starting with a single hygiene kit drive in Charlotte, NC, we set out to prove that organized, student-led action — backed by strong community partnerships — can create real, measurable impact.

Our goal is 20,000 O.L.I Kits. No deadline — we're just getting started.

Our Mission

At the One Love Initiative, we believe students have the power to create real, lasting change. Our mission is to mobilize young people across Charlotte to raise funds, assemble O.L.I Kits, and get essential supplies to individuals and families facing hardship.

Core Values

What drives
everything we do

Student Leadership

We believe students are not just the future — they are the present. Every drive we organize, every kit we assemble, and every partnership we build is led by young people committed to making Charlotte a better place for everyone.

Community First

Every decision we make is guided by empathy and a deep respect for the dignity of the people we serve. We show up not just with supplies, but with genuine care for the individuals and families who receive our O.L.I Kits.

What We Do

From a dollar
to a doorstep

Every O.L.I Kit starts with a dollar raised and ends with a family in need. Here's how we make it happen.

1

Identify Needs

We work with Crisis Assistance Ministry to identify the specific items families in Charlotte need most, ensuring every drive is targeted and impactful.

2

Organize Drives

Students coordinate and lead fundraising efforts at schools, places of worship, and neighborhoods across Charlotte, then we buy the supplies wholesale.

3

Assemble Kits

Volunteers come together to sort the supplies and pack them into O.L.I Kits — ready-to-distribute bundles of essential items.

4

Deliver to Partners

Completed O.L.I Kits are handed off to Crisis Assistance Ministry for direct distribution to families experiencing hardship.

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about the One Love Initiative, our drives, and how you can get involved.

What is the One Love Initiative?
+
The One Love Initiative is a student-led nonprofit based in Charlotte, NC, dedicated to assembling and distributing O.L.I Kits — bundles of essential supplies — for individuals and families experiencing hardship across our community.
What is an O.L.I Kit?
+
An O.L.I Kit is a bundle of essential items bought in bulk and packed by our student volunteers. Our current kit is the Children's Hygiene Kit, which includes a kids toothbrush, toothpaste, kid-friendly soap or body wash, and kid-friendly lotion.
Who receives the kits?
+
All O.L.I Kits are distributed through our partner organization, Crisis Assistance Ministry, which serves individuals and families facing financial hardship in Mecklenburg County, NC.
Can I donate supplies instead of money?
+
We are not running an item-collection drive at the moment. Buying in bulk stretches a dollar much further than collecting individual items, so we raise funds, purchase supplies wholesale, and assemble the kits together as a group. If you'd still like to contribute supplies directly, email us at oneloveinitiative.official@gmail.com and we'll work something out.
How do I donate money?
+
Yes — that's the most useful way to help right now. Financial donations go toward purchasing bulk supplies for O.L.I Kit assembly. You can donate securely through our Stripe donation page — click the Donate button in the navigation bar.
How do I volunteer?
+
We'd love to have you! Volunteers help organize drives, assemble kits, and spread the word. Email us at oneloveinitiative.official@gmail.com or fill out the contact form on our Contact page to get started.
Can I start a drive at my school or organization?
+
Absolutely. We welcome students, school clubs, religious organizations, and community groups to host their own drives. We provide full guidance on how to organize and contribute. Reach out to us to get started.
How are donations used?
+
Donated funds go toward purchasing supplies for O.L.I Kit assembly and the direct costs of running our drives. We are a volunteer operation — nobody draws a salary. If you'd like a breakdown of how a specific donation was spent, just ask.
Where is the One Love Initiative based?
+
We are based in Charlotte, North Carolina, and currently serve families across the greater Charlotte and Mecklenburg County area.
Is my donation tax-deductible?
+
No. The One Love Initiative is not a registered 501(c)(3), so donations to us are not tax-deductible and we cannot issue a deductible receipt. We are working on a fiscal sponsorship that would change this, and we will say so here plainly if and when it is in place.
How do I partner with the One Love Initiative?
+
If your organization would like to partner with us — whether as a distribution partner, sponsor, or community collaborator — please reach out at oneloveinitiative.official@gmail.com. We'd love to connect.

Still Have Questions?

We'd love to hear from you. Reach out and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

Legal

Privacy
Policy

Last updated August 2026. Plain language, no boilerplate — this describes exactly what we collect and why.

Who we are

The One Love Initiative ("OLI", "we") is a student-led volunteer group based in Charlotte, North Carolina. We are not a registered 501(c)(3) organization. You can reach us at oneloveinitiative.official@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect information you type into a form yourself. We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell, rent, or trade anything you give us.

  • Newsletter — your email address, so we can tell you when a drive is scheduled.
  • Volunteer application — your name, email, phone number, age, availability, and the roles you're interested in, so we can contact you about volunteering.
  • Contact form — this one opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is submitted to us until you press send in your email client, and we receive it as an ordinary email.
  • The OLI assistant — the messages you type into the chat window. They are sent to our server and on to Anthropic's API to generate a reply. Please don't put sensitive personal information into the chat.

Where it goes

Newsletter and volunteer submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet, accessible only to OLI organizers. Chat messages are processed by Anthropic to produce a response. Donations are handled entirely by Stripe — payment card details go to Stripe, never to us, and we never see your full card number. This site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard security and traffic logs.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. Cloudflare may set essential cookies for security and performance. Our typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so loading a page makes a request to Google that includes your IP address; that is the only third-party request the site makes on its own.

Young people

We are a student organization, and volunteers under 18 are welcome. If you are under 13, please have a parent or guardian submit the form on your behalf. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information directly, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

Email us at any time to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted — including unsubscribing from the newsletter. We will action the request and confirm when it's done. There is no account to close and nothing to fill out.

Donations and taxes

OLI is not a registered 501(c)(3), and donations to OLI are not tax-deductible. We say so plainly here so nobody gives under the wrong impression.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be mentioned in the newsletter.

Questions About Your Data?
